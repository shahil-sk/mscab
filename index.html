  • Average Distance: ~350 km
  • Travel Time: ~7 to 8 hours

Coastal Express

Mangalore ↔ Udupi & Manipal

4-lane coastal highway run along NH 66. Daily transfers for university students, healthcare visits to KMC Manipal, business meetings, and Sri Krishna Temple darshan.

  • Average Distance: ~58–65 km
  • Travel Time: ~1 hour 15 mins
  • Recommended: Hatchback or Sedan
North Coastal Circuit

Mangalore ↔ Murudeshwar & Gokarna

Scenic coastal route passing Kundapura, Bhatkal, Murudeshwar Shiva Temple, and the beaches of Gokarna. Experienced drivers for relaxed coastal sightseeing.

  • Average Distance: ~160 to 230 km
  • Travel Time: ~3.5 to 4.5 hours
  • Recommended: Innova Crysta SUV

Smooth border highway connection from Mangalore to Kasaragod, Bekal Fort, and northern Kerala backwaters. Quick dispatches with all border permit guidance.
